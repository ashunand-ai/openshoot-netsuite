# OpenShoot NetSuite Academy — Open-source market strategy

## Promise

Reduce dependency on paid SI services for **repeatable NetSuite platform education, native feature adoption, routine configuration discovery, documentation and standard initial validation**. Do not claim to replace external assurance, segregation-of-duties reviews, complex tax/accounting design, bespoke integration or business-critical migration.

## Our differentiation

Oracle publishes release notes, Help topics, its native AI Connector and 48 business SuiteSkills. OpenShoot does not repackage Oracle copyrighted skill code. It combines public feature announcements with independent, account-aware admin learning paths, checklists, synthetic examples, evidence standards and original video instructions.

| What consultants may bill to do | Free self-service alternative | When specialist is still justified |
|---|---|---|
| Explain 2026.2 feature availability | Account-release checklist and sourced tutorial | Contract/licensing/entitlement disputes |
| Walk through native MCP setup | Guided prerequisites, OAuth/role checklist, evidence workflow | Identity governance, external data protection review |
| Teach REST saved searches | Source-linked API walk-through and safe examples | Complex integration and performance architecture |
| Teach dashboards and workbooks | Item pricing workbook and reporting lessons | Audit-grade reconciliation and data modelling |
| Create generic implementation handover | Readiness/evidence reports | Formal security assurance or regulated environment certification |
| Train end users on standard workflows | Short, free, auditable lessons | Business process redesign and organisational change |

## Publishing release gates

**RESEARCHED** = Oracle source has been read and topic prioritised; no complete script.
**SCRIPTED** = source-linked written how-to and recording storyboard exist; no video publication claim.
**RECORDED** = authorised/synthetic footage has been recorded; not yet published or fully QA-checked.
**PUBLISHED** = public video is accessible, manually QA-reviewed and linked in the catalog; if footage is illustrative it is labelled.

No video may show invented live financial results, undisclosed synthetic screens, passwords, access tokens, or third-party customer data.

## Content distribution

1. LinkedIn: 45–90 second feature hook and concise practice takeaway; link directly to open-source guide.
2. YouTube: 4–7 minute step-by-step version after live UI capture and QA.
3. GitHub: permanent reproducible guide, sources, evidence checklist, Skill instruction and limitations.
4. Shorts/Reels: one obstacle, one outcome, one concrete step; no unsupported production-readiness claims.

Every post should describe **what Oracle built**, **what OpenShoot independently provides**, and **what still needs permissions, entitlements or expert review**.

## Metrics to track

- People who open the guide from a unique campaign link
- Tutorials completing editorial QA and live screen capture
- Tutorial completion and actionable feedback
- GitHub stars, forks, issues and accepted PRs
- Verified onboarding tasks completed without SI assistance (opt-in, customer-reported; don't invent savings)
- Reported time saved, with baseline and methodology
