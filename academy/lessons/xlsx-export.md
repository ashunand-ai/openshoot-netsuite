# Export NetSuite saved searches and reports to XLSX in 2026.2

**Audience:** Finance users  
**Oracle release:** 2026.2  
**Status:** Scripted tutorial; not verified in a live NetSuite tenant  
**Risk classification:** read-only

## Why this matters

A small NetSuite 2026.2 update can save finance teams a lot of spreadsheet friction. This walkthrough uses Oracle's published capability and focuses on reducing repetitive professional-services setup or training work. It does not replace complex design, security or audit assurance.

## Before you begin

Confirm the account is upgraded to NetSuite 2026.2 and you can access a permitted report. Access and UI vary by account and role. Use a sandbox or authorised non-sensitive training data; do not share credentials or customer data.

## Step-by-step administrator/user workflow

1. Confirm that the account displays release 2026.2 or later.
2. Open a report, list or saved search your role permits you to export.
3. Use the Excel/export option in the NetSuite UI.
4. Verify the downloaded file uses .xlsx rather than the old default .xls.
5. Check totals, dates, numeric types and currency formatting before sharing.
6. Store exports only in locations approved for customer financial data.

## What good looks like

Verified, access-controlled XLSX export procedure. Ask the user to save an evidence reference, timestamp, applicable role and unresolved blocker in a private location.

## Troubleshooting

If menus or tools are unavailable, first check account release, feature entitlement, role access and the latest Oracle instructions. Do not escalate privileges without administrator approval. If the outcome differs from NetSuite, treat NetSuite source records as authoritative and stop before further actions.
