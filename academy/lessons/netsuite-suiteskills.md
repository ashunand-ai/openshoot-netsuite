# Use Oracle's 48 NetSuite SuiteSkills before buying custom AI workflows

**Audience:** Finance leaders and administrators  
**Oracle release:** 2026.2  
**Status:** Scripted tutorial; not verified in a live NetSuite tenant  
**Risk classification:** read-only

## Why this matters

Before you pay for another bespoke AI workflow, check the 48 NetSuite SuiteSkills Oracle already provides. This walkthrough uses Oracle's published capability and focuses on reducing repetitive professional-services setup or training work. It does not replace complex design, security or audit assurance.

## Before you begin

Check eligible supported AI platform, Oracle SuiteSkills availability, NetSuite role and connector access. Access and UI vary by account and role. Use a sandbox or authorised non-sensitive training data; do not share credentials or customer data.

## Step-by-step administrator/user workflow

1. Verify whether Oracle's SuiteSkills plug-in is available in your supported AI client and account.
2. Review its five categories: Customers & Sales, Finance, Onboarding, Operations and Workforce.
3. Select a low-risk, read-only use case such as a finance summary or pipeline review.
4. Confirm that the connected NetSuite role has access to the required data.
5. Run the available workflow with non-sensitive test data, verify the underlying figures in NetSuite, and document differences.
6. Use explicit approval for any workflow that proposes a record change.

## What good looks like

Choose an Oracle-supported native skill for a permitted business use case. Ask the user to save an evidence reference, timestamp, applicable role and unresolved blocker in a private location.

## Troubleshooting

If menus or tools are unavailable, first check account release, feature entitlement, role access and the latest Oracle instructions. Do not escalate privileges without administrator approval. If the outcome differs from NetSuite, treat NetSuite source records as authoritative and stop before further actions.
